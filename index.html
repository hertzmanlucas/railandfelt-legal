Privacy Policy

Rail & Felt · Last updated 4 August 2026

Rail & Felt is a chip tracker for private, in-person poker games. It records who is at the table and how the chips moved. It is not a gambling app: no money is wagered, held, transferred, or paid out through it.

Who is responsible

Lucas Hertzman. Questions about this policy or your data: u3902254530@id.gle.

What we collect

If you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account identified only by a random ID. We store the display name you type and the game data below. We do not know who you are.

If you create an account, we additionally store your email address. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a salted hash — we never see it.

Game data, for every player: display name, seat position, chip stack, buy-ins, rebuys, cash-outs, the actions taken during each hand, and the settlement calculated at the end of a session.

Subscription data, if you buy premium: whether your entitlement is active, which store it came from, when it expires, and the purchase events our payment provider sends us. We never receive your card details — payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play.

On your device only: your login session, held in the device keychain.

What we do not collect

No analytics or telemetry. No advertising identifiers, ad networks, or third-party trackers. No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share your personal data with anyone for their own purposes.

Who we share it with

Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in; account and game data lives there, in the EU (Ireland).

RevenueCat manages subscription entitlements, and receives your account ID and purchase events.

Apple and Google process payments and report the outcome to RevenueCat.

Other players at your table necessarily see your display name, chip stack, and results — that is the point of a shared chip tracker.

Otherwise we disclose data only where legally required.

How long we keep it

Account and game data is kept until you delete your account. Guest data is kept until the guest identity is cleared from the device — clearing it is irreversible and we cannot recover the history afterwards.

Deleting your account

Account → Delete account, in this app. That permanently removes your account and your personal details.

Because a session's settlement is shared history belonging to everyone who played it, other players' records of that session survive with your name replaced by "Deleted player". Your email, display name, and login are gone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — those are billed by Apple or Google and must be cancelled in your store account settings.

Children

Rail & Felt is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13, or the equivalent minimum age where you live.

Why we are allowed to process it

For account and game data: because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for. There is no chip tracker without a record of the chips.

For subscription data: because it is necessary to give you what you paid for, and because tax law requires records of sales.

We do not rely on consent for anything, because we do not do the things consent is normally needed for — no analytics, no advertising, no marketing email, no profiling.

Your rights

You may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your national data protection authority. In Norway that is Datatilsynet.

The in-app tools cover access (your history and stats), correction (display name), and deletion (Account → Delete account).

For anything else, contact u3902254530@id.gle.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes.
